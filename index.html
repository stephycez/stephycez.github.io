Zicheng Zhu (朱紫橙)

Assistant Professor, School of Informatics
Kyoto University

Hello! My research interest is AI for human well-being, especially social well-being. Questions that particularly interest me are:

  • How does AI affect people’s mental well-being and social connections?
  • How can we design and govern AI to better support human mental well-being and social engagement?

For the sake of my own well-being, I snowboard, dance, play badminton and tennis, watch films, and chat with friends in my spare time.

I was a postdoc at the AI4SG Lab, School of Computing, National University of Singapore, where I worked with Dr. Yi-Chieh Lee. I completed my Ph.D. in the Department of Communications and New Media, National University of Singapore, advised by Dr. Renwen Zhang and Dr. Alex Mitchell.
